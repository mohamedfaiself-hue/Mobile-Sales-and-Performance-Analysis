# 📱 Mobile Sales & Performance Analysis 2026

## 📊 Project Overview

**Mobile Sales & Performance Analysis** is an interactive **Power BI dashboard project** designed to analyze mobile phone brands, models, launch trends, pricing, market distribution, and device specifications.

The dashboard transforms mobile product data into meaningful visual insights using **Power BI, Power Query, DAX, and interactive visualizations**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze mobile models across different brands
- Understand mobile model launch trends over the years
- Compare average India launch prices by brand
- Analyze price distribution and price segments
- Explore mobile models by country
- Compare mobile specifications such as RAM, screen size, battery, and cameras
- Provide an interactive model-level exploration experience

## 📑 Dashboard Pages

### 1️⃣ Overview

Provides a high-level summary of the mobile dataset.

**Includes:**
- Total Models
- Total Brands
- Average India Price
- Average RAM
- Average Battery
- Mobile Models Launched by Year
- Brand, Year and Processor filters

---

### 2️⃣ Brand Trend

Analyzes mobile brands and their launch patterns.

**Includes:**
- Mobile Models by Brand
- Brand-wise Model Launch Trend
- Brand filtering
- Comparison of model portfolios across brands

---

### 3️⃣ Price Analysis

Provides detailed analysis of mobile pricing.

**Includes:**
- Average India Price by Brand
- Average Price by Year
- Mobile Price by Country
- Models by Price Segment
- Model, Year and Brand filters

**Price Segments:**
- Below 15K
- 15K–30K
- 30K–50K
- Above 50K

---

### 4️⃣ Market Performance

Provides a detailed view of mobile products and market-level distribution.

**Includes:**
- Company Name
- Model Name
- Country
- Price
- India Launch Price
- Launch Year
- Top Models by India Launch Price
- Model Distribution by Country

---

### 5️⃣ Specification Analysis

Analyzes the technical specifications of mobile devices.

**Includes:**
- RAM Distribution
- Screen Size Distribution
- Processor Distribution
- Battery Capacity Distribution
- Front Camera Distribution
- Back Camera Distribution

---

### 6️⃣ Mobile Explorer

Provides model-level interactive analysis.

Users can filter by:

- Model Name
- Launch Year
- Screen Size
- Battery Capacity

The selected model's details include:

- Launch Year
- Front Camera
- Back Camera
- RAM
- Processor
- Battery
- Other specifications

## 🔍 Key Insights

- The dataset contains **901 mobile models across 19 brands**.
- Brand portfolios vary significantly in the number of models represented.
- India launch prices show substantial variation across brands and years.
- Mobile models are distributed across multiple price segments.
- The dashboard allows comparison of hardware specifications across a wide range of devices.
- Interactive slicers help users move from overall market analysis to individual model-level analysis.
